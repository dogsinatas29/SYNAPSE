# Phase 9 - Acceptance Test

## Purpose

Phase9 validates the complete collaborative workflow.

Phase9 introduces no new functionality.

Phase9 verifies that all previous phases operate together as a complete system.

```text
Phase2
        ↓
Phase3
        ↓
Phase4
        ↓
Phase5
        ↓
Phase6
        ↓
Phase7
        ↓
Phase8
```

The entire pipeline must execute successfully.

---

# Goal

Verify end-to-end collaborative operation.

Confirm that:

* Identity works
* Session management works
* Submission works
* Visualization works
* Review works
* Verification works
* Remote Edit works
* Harvest works
* Boundary Enforcement works

---

# Core Principle

Phase9 is not a feature phase.

Phase9 is a validation phase.

```text
Build
        ↓
Validate
```

Every major workflow must complete successfully.
