
# v0.3.34.22 Ontology Specification
## SYNAPSE Architectural Physics

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Architectural Ontology used by SYNAPSE.

The ontology serves as the foundation of all architectural reasoning.

Every detector, classifier, rule engine, report generator, and validation system must derive its conclusions from concepts defined here.

Without a stable ontology:

```text
Authority
Boundary
Criticality
Control
Extension
```

become subjective interpretations.

With a stable ontology:

```text
Observation
↓
Evidence
↓
Ontology
↓
Reasoning
↓
Finding
↓
Validation
```

becomes reproducible.

---

# Architectural Reasoning Principle

SYNAPSE does not reason directly from files.

SYNAPSE reasons from architectural concepts.

Example:

```text
GraphModel.ts
```

is not meaningful.

Instead:

```text
GraphModel
↓
State Owner
↓
Authority
↓
Power Center
```

is meaningful.

Architectural reasoning operates on concepts, not files.

---

# Ontology Structure

Every ontology entity must define:

```text
Definition

Required Evidence

Derived Metrics

Related Queries
```

# Authority

## Definition

A component that influences or controls system behavior.

Authority is determined by ownership of decisions, state, policies, or execution paths.

Authority is not equivalent to popularity.

Authority is not equivalent to dependency count.

---

## Observable Evidence

```text
State Ownership

Policy Ownership

Command Ownership

Execution Ownership

Dependency Influence
```

---

## Counter Examples

```text
Logger

Utils

Theme Constants

Configuration Objects
```

High dependency count alone does not create authority.

---

## Required Evidence

Minimum:

```text
2 independent authority indicators
```

---

## Derived Metrics

```text
Authority Score

State Score

Policy Score

Control Score
```

---

## Produced Findings

```text

Dominant Authority
```

---

## Related Queries

```text

What causes failure?
```

# Final Rule

All architectural reasoning must follow:

```text
Evidence
↓
Ontology
↓
Reasoning Rule
↓
Finding
↓
Confidence
↓
Validation
↓
Recommendation
```

Any conclusion that bypasses this chain is invalid.

The ontology defined in this document is the canonical architectural vocabulary of SYNAPSE.
