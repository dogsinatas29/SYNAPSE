This plan transitions SYNAPSE into the Re-engineering phase. The absolute priority is survival: eradicating the object amplification explosion and the false positive graph pollution.

Success Criteria (Strict Enforcement)
CAUTION

Single Success Criteria:

Amplification Reduction: Target Amplification <20x (>80% reduction from 190x baseline)
